About Catfishing Basics

Purpose

I created this website originally as my final project for CMPA-3301 at Texas Tech University but, it has turned into much more to me than just a project. My purpose with this site is to educate the current and future generations of anglers on the best and most effective techniques of catfishing. From the rod and reel all the way to where you should cast your line, this site serves as a sort of catfishing handbook. I hope to educate current anglers, and inspire the anglers of the future.

Values

As an outdoorsman and angler, I value accountability and doing the right thing even when no one is looking. What this means is if you are fishing by yourself, do not simply leave your line clippings or trash for others to pick up. By keeping yourself accountable and doing the right thing, even if it's "harder," we can all help to conserve this gorgeous planet. A good rule of thumb is leave the place better than you found it. This is an elementary teaching in scouting. The only thing you should be leaving outdoors is footprints.

Get in Action

I encourage everyone who reads this to take some time and step outdoors. Whether it be for a weekend camping trip or just 10 minutes for some fresh air. While doing this, take a minute and just look at your surroundings. Do you see trees? Buildings? Roads? While you are outside, pick up some trash. I can almost guarantee that within your first few steps outside, you will see trash. Simply pick it up and and throw it away. If you are out fishing, collect the trash that the others before you left. Step up for yourself and for the Earth.
